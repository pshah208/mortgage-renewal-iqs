## 1. Chair's framing

Diane Lafleur opened by stating that the campaign is "**directionally working but being solved file by file when the problems are structural**." She identified three structural problems that have been raised repeatedly by branch leadership and directed that these — not the individual escalations — constitute the executive agenda:

1. **Advisor capacity at BR-101 (Toronto Bay & Bloor)**
2. **Competitor pricing pressure in Québec (BR-202)**
3. **Payment shock among self-employed clients in Calgary (BR-303)**

Everything else raised in the last six weeks was characterised as a symptom of one of these three.

## 2. Position review

Raj Balakrishnan reported:
- ~CAD 22M maturing inside 180 days; ~50 files across the three pilot branches.
- Following the 20 July Council decisions, advisor discretion at 20 bps has closed four BR-101 files that would previously have escalated.
- A manual value-at-risk cut of the renewal list immediately surfaced three high-exposure files that were not being worked.
- Outstanding: floating advisor not yet assigned (HR); amortization fast-path form not yet published (Credit Risk); broker-originated renewals still unowned (13 files, ~CAD 5M across three branches).

Derek Fontaine reported that the Québec evidence pack (11 competitor sheets from BR-202) is sufficient to support a regional grid recommendation; median competitor 5-year fixed on comparable uninsured renewal profiles sits ~34 bps below the current national floor, with two caisses at ~41 bps below on relationship clients. Separately noted that one monoline is not competing on rate but on a 30-year amortization for straight renewals, which is winning self-employed business.

## 3. Executive guidance issued

Diane Lafleur issued the following direction, to be actioned within 60 days.

**3.1 Pricing.** Derek Fontaine to bring a **Québec regional grid recommendation — a recommendation, not options** — to the Renewal Council of 10 August. The evidence pack is deemed sufficient. If the recommendation is a 20 bps regional floor, it is to be stated as such and decided.

**3.2 Credit.** Credit Risk to publish the **amortization relief fast path by 7 August**. The CRO's three conditions are the boundary and are not to be stretched: *same borrower, no increase in principal, no extension beyond the original contractual amortization remaining.*

**3.3 Capacity.** The **BR-101 floating advisor is to be assigned this week**; Raj Balakrishnan to unblock with HR personally.

**3.4 Ownership.** **Broker-originated renewals receive a named owner by Friday.** Assigned to the Broker Channel lead. Thirteen files with no owner was described by the chair as "an unforced error."

**3.5 Prioritisation.** Value-at-risk sorted renewal lists to go live on the 1st. Marcus Delaney's warning is formally noted: **a low attrition score suppresses urgency, not the relationship.** No client with a material deposit balance is to go uncontacted on the basis of a model score.

**3.6 Reporting.** The CRO's monthly renewal pack requirement is **approved**: retained volume split by **LTV band** and **payment-shock band**, plus confirmation that the attrition model has completed validation.

**3.7 Margin discipline.** The chair restated: *"Retention over margin for relationship clients — but that is not a licence to buy the book. If we are giving away more than 20 basis points on average across the campaign, come and tell me, because that means the grid is wrong and the answer is to fix the grid, not to keep discounting."*

## 4. Risk position — Chief Risk Officer

Karen Whitfield recorded four conditions on continued support for the campaign:

1. **Exception volume is a risk indicator in itself.** 41 exceptions across three branches in one quarter means the grid has effectively been moved without governance. Fixing the grid is the correct remedy.
2. **Amortization fast path is narrowly bounded.** Breaching any of the three conditions converts a straight renewal into a new underwriting decision, at which point **OSFI Guideline B-20** expectations for income verification and total debt-service testing apply in full.
3. **Concentration must be monitored.** Retaining a high-LTV, high-payment-shock cohort at discounted pricing concentrates precisely the exposure that B-20 portfolio risk limits exist to control. Reporting by LTV band and payment-shock band is required, not retention rate alone.
4. **Model governance.** `attrition-renewal-v2.3` drives client-facing offers and is therefore in scope under **OSFI Guideline E-23** (model risk management). Required: completed validation, ability to explain a score to a client on request, and confirmation that no prohibited attribute is used directly or by proxy.

## 5. Compliance position

Helena Vasquez recorded:
- **French-language disclosure.** The stale French rate table on the renewal portal is a disclosure defect; the date the French table was last accurate must be established to size remediation.
- **Fair treatment.** Approved language and a referral path are required for property-sale enquiries; the rationale field on discretionary pricing must be mandatory to prevent differential discounting on the basis of negotiation persistence.
- **Script review.** The contact-centre renewal script must be reviewed by Compliance before it is scaled beyond the BR-101 pilot.
- **Privacy.** A privacy assessment is required on the behavioural signals feeding the attrition model.

## 6. Decisions not taken

- A **blanket competitor rate match** remains rejected.
- A **national floor reduction** was not approved; the response to Québec pressure is regional, evidence-based and governed.
