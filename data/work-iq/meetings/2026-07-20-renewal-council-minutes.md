## 1. Campaign position

Raj opened with the book: approximately **CAD 22M of balance maturing inside 180 days** across the three pilot branches, spread over roughly 50 files. The vintage is 2021–2023 originations at 1.8–3.2%, renewing into an offered band of 4.1–5.3%. Retention in the last completed quarter was 78–92% by branch.

Diane restated the three non-negotiables from the June kickoff: **retention over margin on relationship clients**, **no pricing below guardrail without documented competitor evidence**, and **proactive contact at 120 days, not 30**.

## 2. Branch leader concerns tabled

**Marcus Delaney (BR-101, Toronto Bay & Bloor) — capacity.**
18 maturities inside 180 days against two advisors carrying ~70 active files each. The 120-day proactive contact standard is not achievable at that ratio. Mass Market renewals are going untouched until the automated 30-day maturity letter. One 640k file was lost last month because first contact happened at day 22. Requested either a floating advisor or authority to route sub-300k renewals to the contact centre with a scripted offer.

**Sophie Tremblay (BR-202, Montréal Plateau) — competitor pricing.**
Local caisses and two credit unions quoting **30–40 bps below** CFC Bank's posted-less-discount rate, advertised locally. Clients arriving with printed competitor sheets. Advisor discretion of 15 bps is insufficient; conversations end in "let me escalate" and the client leaves undecided. Two of four such clients subsequently requested payout statements. Separately flagged that ~40% of the branch's renewal book prefers French and the French renewal portal is showing a stale rate table.

**Nadia Osei (BR-303, Calgary Centre Street) — payment shock.**
Concentration of self-employed clients originated 2021–22 near 2% on 25-year amortizations, renewing at 4.8–5.2%, producing **payment increases of 70–130%**. Three clients have raised selling the property. Two requested amortization extension toward 30 years; credit re-test turnaround is running 6–9 business days against monolines that will complete a straight renewal without re-verifying income.

## 5. Decisions

1. **Advisor discretion raised 15 → 20 bps** nationally for renewals inside 180 days. **Branch Manager authority raised to 25 bps** with same-day turnaround. Above 25 bps remains **Pricing Committee**.
2. **Evidence standard set.** Up to 20 bps: dated screenshot or advisor-attested verbal quote, noted on file. Above 20 bps: written competitor commitment on letterhead or lender-portal PDF, mandatory.
3. **Amortization relief fast path approved** for straight renewals, on the CRO's conditions: same borrower, no increase in principal, no extension beyond the original contractual amortization remaining. Credit Risk to publish the fast path. Haddad's three files are the pilot.
4. **Renewal lists re-sorted by value at risk** (balance × attrition probability), with payment-shock and relationship-depth columns. Analytics to deliver for the 1st.
5. **Québec pricing** to be addressed as a **regional grid proposal**, not further one-off exceptions. Tremblay to supply competitor evidence pack; Fontaine to bring a recommendation to the 10 August Council.
6. **BR-101 capacity**: floating advisor funded centrally for Q3; in exchange BR-101 pilots contact-centre handling of sub-300k renewals with a scripted offer, reporting back in four weeks.

**Explicitly not approved:** a blanket competitor rate match.

## 6. Risk and compliance conditions

Karen Whitfield (CRO):
- Exception **volume** is itself a risk indicator; normalising exceptions moves the grid without governance.
- Amortization fast path is bounded by the three conditions above; breaching any one makes it a **new underwriting decision** with full OSFI **B-20** income-verification and debt-service testing.
- Monthly renewal pack must report retained volume split by **LTV band** and **payment-shock band**, not retention rate alone, to monitor concentration against B-20 portfolio limits.
- The attrition model (`attrition-renewal-v2.3`) drives client offers and is therefore in scope for **OSFI E-23** model risk management: validation evidence required, scores must be explainable to a client, no prohibited attribute used directly or by proxy.

Helena Vasquez (Compliance):
- French renewal portal showing a stale rate table is a **disclosure defect**, not a UX bug; remediation to be sized.
- Advisors improvising responses to "should I sell?" approaches unlicensed advice; approved language and a referral path required.
- Differential discounting without a recorded rationale is a **fair-treatment** exposure; the rationale field becomes mandatory.
- Contact-centre renewal script to be reviewed by Compliance before scaling.
