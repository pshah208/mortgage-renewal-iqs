## Item 1 — RNW-5014, discretionary pricing exception

**Requested by:** Wei Zhang (ADV-021), endorsed by Sophie Tremblay (BM, BR-202)

**File summary**

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Balance | CAD 612,000 |
| Product | 5-year fixed closed |
| Days to maturity | 41 |
| Client tenure | 11 years |
| Products held | Chequing, line of credit, locked-in RRSP |
| Household deposits | ~CAD 190,000 |
| Attrition band | High |
| Guardrail floor for profile | 4.94% |
| Competitor written offer | 4.62% |
| Discount requested | 32 bps |

**Authority analysis.** The relationship-bundle offer (OFR-02) caps at 20 bps at Branch Manager level. Requests above 25 bps sit with the Pricing Committee under the discretionary pricing approval matrix. The request is therefore correctly before this committee and could not have been approved at Director level.

**Discussion.** Treasury noted that at 28 bps the file remains net-interest-margin positive over the term on a fully-loaded funds-transfer-price basis; at 35 bps it would not. Credit Risk confirmed no adverse credit indicators and an LTV inside portfolio limits. Karen Whitfield questioned whether matching to within 4 bps of a competitor is materially less effective than matching exactly; the committee accepted that an 11-year relationship with a deposit anchor does not require a to-the-basis-point match, and that establishing a precedent of exact matching would invite escalation on every file.

**Decision — APPROVED at 28 bps (not 32), conditional.**

Conditions:
1. The competitor offer must be attached to the file as a PDF. A screenshot does not satisfy the documentation standard for an exception of this size.
2. The client's chequing account must remain the primary payment account for the full term. Migration triggers review of the discount at the next anniversary.
3. The exception is logged and will appear in the quarterly pricing exception report to Risk.

**Recorded rationale:** relationship value materially exceeds the foregone mortgage margin; documented competitor evidence present; attrition band High; deposit anchor at risk.

## Item 2 — Exception volume, standing concern

Derek Fontaine tabled that **41 discretionary pricing exceptions** were granted across BR-101, BR-202 and BR-303 in the last completed quarter, of which **6 originated from BR-202 in the current quarter alone**.

Committee position, recorded:
> Where a branch or region requires repeated exceptions of a similar size and shape, the correct remedy is a **grid change with governance**, not a sustained pattern of individual approvals. A high volume of individually-defensible exceptions constitutes an undocumented change to the pricing framework and is a control weakness in its own right.

**Action:** Fontaine to prepare a Québec regional grid recommendation for the Renewal Council of 10 August, supported by branch-supplied competitor evidence. Interim: BR-202 may use Branch Manager authority of 25 bps on the three files maturing inside 30 days (RNW-5008, RNW-5021, RNW-5044) rather than escalating each individually.

## Item 3 — Evidence standard for competitor offers

Raised via advisor escalation (Jonas Berg, ADV-032) on RNW-5033: a 22 bps request supported only by an unattributed phone screenshot.

Committee recommendation to Renewal Council (subsequently adopted):

| Discount sought | Acceptable evidence |
|---|---|
| ≤ 20 bps | Dated screenshot **or** advisor-attested verbal quote, recorded in the file rationale field |
| > 20 bps | Written competitor commitment on letterhead **or** lender-portal PDF — mandatory |

Karen Whitfield added that the **rationale field must be mandatory** at every discount level. Two clients with the same risk profile receiving different pricing on the basis of negotiation persistence alone, with no recorded rationale, is a fair-treatment exposure.

## Item 4 — Amortization relief and its pricing interaction

Referred from BR-303 (three self-employed files, combined ~CAD 1.6M). The committee's pricing view only; the credit decision sits with Credit Risk.

- Amortization relief is **not** a pricing concession and must not be counted against discretionary discount limits.
- However, relief and a rate discount **may not be stacked without Branch Manager sign-off**, to prevent the two levers being used sequentially by different approvers on the same file.
- Committee supports Credit Risk's fast path for straight renewals subject to the CRO conditions (same borrower, no increase in principal, no extension beyond the original contractual amortization remaining).

## Item 5 — Campaign-level guardrail

At the request of the EVP, Personal Banking, the committee records a campaign-level monitoring threshold:

> If the **average discount granted across the renewal campaign exceeds 20 bps**, the matter escalates to the EVP, Personal Banking. An average beyond that level indicates the posted grid, not the individual decisions, requires correction.
