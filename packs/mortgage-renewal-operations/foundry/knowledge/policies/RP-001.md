# RP-001 - Renewal Pricing Guardrails - National Discount Grid

- **Effective date:** 2026-07-22
- **Owner:** Director, Mortgage Pricing
- **Category:** pricing_guardrail

The guardrail floor is the posted term rate less the maximum cumulative
discount permitted for the profile. Pricing below the floor requires a Pricing
Committee exception under RP-002.

During the campaign, Mortgage Advisors may approve up to 20 basis points and
Branch Managers up to 25 basis points with same-day authority. Regional
Directors may approve up to 35 basis points for High Net Worth clients.
Requests above 25 basis points for other segments go to the Pricing Committee
and must remain net-interest-margin positive on a fully loaded funds-transfer
price basis.

Discounts do not stack beyond the approving authority's limit. Amortization
relief under RP-006 is not a pricing concession, but combining it with a rate
discount requires Branch Manager sign-off. A campaign average above 20 basis
points escalates to the EVP, Personal Banking. Blanket competitor matching is
not authorized; regional variations follow RP-008.
