# RP-010 - Renewal Escalation Procedure and Service Standards

- **Effective date:** 2026-07-22
- **Owner:** VP, Real Estate Secured Lending
- **Category:** approval_requirement

Escalate when the request exceeds authority, an offer does not fit the
segment, amortization relief is outside the fast path, or a conduct,
complaint, or vulnerability concern is raised.

The escalation must contain renewal identifier, balance, product, term, days
to maturity, tenure, products and relationship balances relied upon, risk
band and drivers, guardrail floor, discount sought, evidence, expiry date,
and rationale. Branch Manager decisions are same day, Regional Director one
business day, Pricing Committee two business days, and Credit Risk fast-path
decisions two business days from a complete submission.

Every outcome records approver, amount, conditions, and monitoring. Three or
more substantially similar files in a quarter become a pattern escalation.
Splitting requests across approvers, using amortization relief to evade
pricing authority, or presenting an unapproved offer are control breaches.
