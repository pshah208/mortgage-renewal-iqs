# RP-008 - Regional Pricing Variation - Governance and Evidence

- **Effective date:** 2026-07-22
- **Owner:** Mortgage Pricing Committee
- **Category:** pricing_guardrail

A sustained regional competitive difference is addressed through a governed
regional grid, not serial exceptions. Review is triggered by more than five
similar exceptions in a branch or region over a rolling quarter, a median
comparable competitor rate more than 25 basis points below the national
floor, or a documented structural competitor product difference.

The proposal requires at least ten dated comparable competitor quotes matched
on term, insurance status, LTV band, and segment. The Director, Mortgage
Pricing brings one recommendation to the Renewal Council. The Pricing
Committee recommends, the EVP, Personal Banking approves a regional floor,
and Treasury confirms positive fully loaded net interest margin. During review,
Branch Manager authority may be used on named files maturing within 30 days.
An approved variation lasts at most two quarters unless refreshed evidence
supports re-approval.
