# RP-004 - OSFI Guideline B-20 - Application to Mortgage Renewals

- **Effective date:** 2026-01-01
- **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer
- **Category:** regulatory_reference

For a renewal with the same borrower, no principal increase, and no extension
past the original contractual amortization remaining, the bank may treat the
transaction as a straight renewal without full re-underwriting, income
re-verification, or debt-service retesting.

If the borrower changes, principal increases, amortization extends beyond the
original remaining term, or additional funds are advanced, the transaction is
a new underwriting decision and B-20 income verification, debt-service testing,
and property valuation expectations apply in full. LTV above 80 percent
requires mortgage default insurance and renewal pricing cannot bypass LTV
policy. Monthly reporting must split retained volume by LTV and payment-shock
bands; retention rate alone is insufficient. The file must evidence that the
straight-renewal conditions were tested.
