# RP-006 - Amortization Relief Fast Path for Straight Renewals

- **Effective date:** 2026-08-07
- **Owner:** Credit Risk
- **Category:** approval_requirement

The fast path requires all three conditions: same borrower, no increase in
principal, and no extension beyond the original contractual amortization
remaining. If any condition fails, full income verification and total and
gross debt-service testing apply under RP-004.

The account must be current with no arrears in the preceding twelve months and
within LTV policy. Credit Risk approves the change; a rate discount on the same
file also requires Branch Manager sign-off. The service standard is two
business days from a complete submission. The client must receive written
disclosure of additional interest cost, not only the reduced payment. Volume
and portfolio amortization changes are reported monthly to the Chief Risk
Officer.
