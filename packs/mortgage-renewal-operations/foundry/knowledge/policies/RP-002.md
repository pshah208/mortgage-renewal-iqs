# RP-002 - Discretionary Pricing Approval Matrix and Competitor Evidence Standard

- **Effective date:** 2026-07-22
- **Owner:** Mortgage Pricing Committee
- **Category:** approval_requirement

| Discount sought | Approver | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 20 bps | Mortgage Advisor | Immediate, documented |
| 21-25 bps | Branch Manager | Same day |
| 26 bps and above | Pricing Committee | Two business days; out-of-cycle allowed for expiring commitments |
| High Net Worth, up to 35 bps | Regional Director | One business day |
| Any amount with amortization relief | Pricing approver plus Branch Manager | Same day |

Up to 20 basis points requires a dated competitor screenshot or
advisor-attested verbal quote recorded in the rationale field. Above 20 basis
points requires a written competitor commitment on letterhead or a lender
portal PDF attached to the file; a screenshot is insufficient. The rationale
field is mandatory at every level. Conditions must be recorded and monitored,
and exceptions above advisor discretion are reported to Risk. Repeated
similar exceptions require a governed grid review under RP-008.
