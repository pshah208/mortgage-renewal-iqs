OFR-02 requires three or more products including a chequing account. OFR-04
requires written clawback disclosure. OFR-05 requires verified investable
assets of at least CAD 1M held at the bank. OFR-07 follows RP-006 and requires
retesting outside the straight-renewal fast path. OFR-08 requires evidence
meeting the above-20-basis-point standard. Segment assignment comes from the
governed customer master.
