# RP-007 - Fair Treatment of Clients at Renewal - Conduct and Disclosure

- **Effective date:** 2026-07-24
- **Owner:** Director, Regulatory Compliance
- **Category:** risk_consideration

Materially similar risk and relationship profiles should receive materially
similar pricing, with differences supported by objective recorded factors.
Clients must be told clearly what happens if they take no action, including
that the mortgage rolls to a posted rate. Renewal communications must show the
new payment and change from the current payment, with term payments side by
side where applicable.

Advisors are not licensed to advise whether a client should sell property.
Approved language and referral to advice-qualified staff are required. English
and French information must be equivalent and current; a stale rate table in
one language is a disclosure defect. Clients facing payment difficulty must
be offered the full relief set before any property-disposal discussion.
Compliance reviews scripts, handouts, and automated communications before
deployment or scale-up.
