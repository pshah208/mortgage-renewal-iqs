# RP-009 - Renewal Portfolio Risk Considerations and Concentration Limits

- **Effective date:** 2026-07-23
- **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer
- **Category:** risk_consideration

Retention preserves the underlying credit exposure. The monthly renewal pack
must report retained volume and balance by LTV band, payment-shock band,
uninsured share, average discount by branch and segment, exception count and
value, and fast-path amortization approvals with the resulting amortization
profile shift.

Relationship value must be evidenced by deposits, investments, or other
products relied upon. The mandatory rationale, tiered evidence standard, and
quarterly exception report are compensating controls for discount pressure.
Pricing must be published from one authoritative source with an effective date
and version history. Broker-originated renewals require a named owner and a
distinct playbook because their contactability and maturity behavior differ.
