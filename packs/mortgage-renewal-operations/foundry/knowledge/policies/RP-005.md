# RP-005 - OSFI Guideline E-23 - Model Risk Management for the Attrition Model

- **Effective date:** 2026-05-01
- **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer
- **Category:** regulatory_reference

The attrition model is in scope for model risk management because it
prioritizes advisor contact and influences client-facing offers. Before
production use it requires independent validation, conceptual soundness and
benchmarking, explainability of principal drivers, prohibited-attribute and
proxy testing, performance and drift monitoring, and a privacy assessment of
behavioral inputs.

The score informs prioritization; it does not authorize omission or
differential treatment. Users must be able to override a recommendation with
recorded rationale, and override rates are monitored as a model signal.
