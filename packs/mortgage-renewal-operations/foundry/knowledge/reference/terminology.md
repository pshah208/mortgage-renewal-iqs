# Renewal terminology

- **Maturity window:** renewals whose maturity date falls within the selected
  planning horizon.
- **Payment shock:** modeled percentage change in payment under the renewal
  terms; it is not a credit decision.
- **Attrition risk score:** derived probability-like signal used for
  prioritization; always paired with score date and model version.
- **Revenue exposure:** annual revenue at risk under the governed model.
- **Value at Risk:** governed probability-weighted exposure measure.
- **Guardrail floor:** lowest permitted price before the policy exception path.
- **Straight renewal:** same borrower, no principal increase, and no extension
  beyond the original contractual amortization remaining, subject to policy.
- **Regional grid variation:** governed change supported by dated comparable
  evidence, not a collection of informal exceptions.
