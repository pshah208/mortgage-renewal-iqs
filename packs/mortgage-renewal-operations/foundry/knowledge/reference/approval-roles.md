# Approval roles

| Role | Typical authority |
|---|---|
| Mortgage Advisor | Advisor-level discretionary pricing within the active limit |
| Branch Manager | Branch-level exception authority and required combined-lever sign-off |
| Regional Director | Eligible high-net-worth pricing within the catalogue |
| Pricing Committee | Higher discretionary pricing, exceptions, and grid recommendations |
| Credit Risk | Amortization relief and underwriting decisions |
| Compliance | Conduct, disclosure, language equivalence, and scripts |
| EVP, Personal Banking | Campaign or regional grid approval when policy requires |

These are role boundaries, not a delegation for the assistant. The assistant
prepares evidence and names the accountable role.
