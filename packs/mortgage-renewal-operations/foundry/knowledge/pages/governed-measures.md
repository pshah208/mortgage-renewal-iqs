# Governed measures and denominators

Use the assigned semantic model for numeric answers. Core measures include
renewals in the maturity window, balance maturing, annual revenue exposure,
Value at Risk, high-risk renewals, average renewal likelihood, payment-shock
bands, LTV bands, branch performance, and offer coverage.

**Value at Risk** is a governed measure: revenue exposure multiplied by the
point-in-time attrition risk score and aggregated over the filtered population.
It is not a source column and should not be recreated from a partial result.

Every answer must state the denominator and filter context. A branch count,
balance, exposure, or risk total is meaningful only for the same maturity
window, cutoff, and population. Do not compare a current partial period with
complete prior periods without labeling the limitation.

Exposure and risk are different dimensions. A high-risk file may not be the
largest exposure, and a high-balance or low-risk relationship may still merit
contact. Use the requested measure first, then explain the trade-off.
