# Mortgage renewal operations

A mortgage renewal is a time-bounded relationship and risk-management process,
not a standalone rate quote. The operational unit is a mortgage renewal within
the maturity window, joined to customer relationship context, a governed
retention catalogue, and a point-in-time risk signal.

The campaign objective is to retain suitable relationships while preserving
pricing discipline, fair treatment, disclosure quality, and human
accountability. The next-180-day window is a planning population, not a
guarantee that every renewal will be contacted, retained, or eligible for an
offer.

The model separates:

1. Operational facts: maturity, balance, term, payment shock, stage, branch,
   and relationship fields as of a stated date.
2. Derived signals: attrition score, risk band, likelihood, exposure, and
   recommended offer with a score date and model version.
3. Policy constraints: eligible offers, evidence, approval, conduct, and
   regulatory controls.
4. Human decisions: suitability, advice, pricing approval, exceptions, and
   final customer communication.
