# Source authorities and evidence

**Fabric IQ** owns governed numeric facts and measures from the renewal
portfolio, risk summary, offer coverage, and branch performance products.
Use it for counts, balances, bands, exposure, maturity filters, and model
outputs.

**Work IQ** owns campaign correspondence, Teams discussions, and meeting
records. Use it for dated human statements, decisions, concerns, escalations,
and commitments. A statement is not a validated numeric fact until Fabric
corroborates it.

**Foundry knowledge** owns the approved fictional policies and procedures.
Use it for guardrails, authority, eligibility, evidence standards, conduct,
disclosure, and regulatory references.

When sources disagree, preserve the disagreement. Record which source is
observed, which value is derived, the date of each, and the human decision that
remains open.
