# Cutoff and citation procedure

1. Record the requested or default cutoff before retrieval.
2. Filter operational and Work IQ evidence to dates on or before that cutoff.
3. Confirm that all joined facts use compatible `as_of_date` or `scored_on`
   values.
4. Label forward-looking maturity or likelihood results as projections.
5. Cite the exact policy file, source authority, date, and model version used.
6. If a source is missing, stale, or internally inconsistent, stop the
   affected calculation and state the limitation.

Never cite a deployment endpoint, cloud identifier, secret, or guessed URL.
