# Pricing evidence and escalation procedure

An escalation must include the renewal identifier, product and term, balance,
days to maturity, tenure and relationship context relied upon, risk band and
drivers, guardrail floor, discount sought, competitor evidence, expiry date,
and recorded rationale.

Use the approval matrix in `policies/RP-002.md`. Up to the advisor limit,
documented evidence and rationale are required. Above the 20-basis-point
threshold, attach a written competitor commitment on letterhead or a lender
portal PDF. A screenshot alone is not sufficient.

Branch Manager, Regional Director, Pricing Committee, and Credit Risk service
standards are policy-controlled. Repeated similar exceptions are a pattern
escalation for a governed grid review, not permission for more one-offs.
