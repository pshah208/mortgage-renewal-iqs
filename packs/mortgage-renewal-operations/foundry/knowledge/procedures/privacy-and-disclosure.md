# Privacy and disclosure procedure

Before sharing an output, identify the audience and minimize the fields:

- Management and planning: aggregate by branch, segment, band, channel, or
  maturity bucket.
- Authorized operations: only the file fields needed for the stated action.
- Customer-facing material: use approved language and the authorized workflow;
  do not generate a binding quote.

Do not disclose or infer credit scores, late payments, income, protected
attributes, exact addresses, account numbers, or personal circumstances that
are not required and authorized. Do not expose names or relationship balances
in reusable examples.

Every renewal communication must explain payment change, term choices,
consequence of inaction, and material conditions. English and French
communications must be equivalent and current. Compliance approval is required
before scripts, handouts, or automated communications are deployed or scaled.
