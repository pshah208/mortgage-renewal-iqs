# Maturity-window prioritization playbook

1. Fix the cutoff and maturity horizon, normally the next 180 days.
2. Retrieve the governed renewal population and state count, balance, and
   exposure denominators.
3. Rank by the requested decision measure. For relationship prioritization,
   use governed Value at Risk or exposure; do not substitute balance.
4. Add risk band, payment shock, days to maturity, stage, relationship depth,
   and branch capacity as context.
5. Ensure low-risk files remain in the contact population. A low score changes
   urgency, not the obligation to contact.
6. Surface untouched files near maturity and route capacity constraints to the
   branch or operations owner.
7. Prepare, but do not approve, the next action with its evidence and approval
   path.

The output should identify the population, cutoff, ranking measure, exceptions,
and the accountable human owner.
