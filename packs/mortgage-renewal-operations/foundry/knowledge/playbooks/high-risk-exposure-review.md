# High-risk and exposure review playbook

Use this playbook when leadership asks where the book is most exposed.

- Start with governed Value at Risk and revenue exposure for the selected
  maturity population.
- Break results by branch, segment, LTV band, payment-shock band, channel, and
  risk band as requested.
- Explain the tension between high risk and high exposure; do not conflate
  them.
- Flag concentration and retention activity that could preserve high-LTV or
  high-payment-shock exposure.
- Report only the minimum file-level fields needed for an authorized action.
- Route a proposed price, offer, or amortization change to the role specified
  by policy.

The result is a worklist and risk discussion, not a credit or retention
decision.
