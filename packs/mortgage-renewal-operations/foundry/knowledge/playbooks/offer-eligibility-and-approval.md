# Offer eligibility and approval playbook

For each proposed offer, verify in order:

1. The segment comes from the governed customer master.
2. The offer exists in the approved catalogue.
3. The requested discount or cash value is within the offer cap.
4. Eligibility conditions, evidence, and disclosure requirements are met.
5. The highest required approval authority is identified.
6. Any combined lever, such as rate plus amortization relief, receives the
   additional sign-off required by policy.
7. The rationale and conditions are recorded in the authorized workflow.

Do not present an offer outside the catalogue, stack discounts to bypass an
authority limit, or infer eligibility from an advisor's judgment. If the
request does not fit, prepare a policy exception or grid-change escalation.
